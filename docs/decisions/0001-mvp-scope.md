# 0001: Topic-alert MVP and operating constraints

Status: superseded in part by [0002](0002-web-first-mvp.md)
Date: 2026-10-19
Related ideas: IDEA-001, IDEA-002

## Context
The maintainer clarified the primary user outcome and delivery constraints during planning.

## Decision
Launch topic following and alerts across City of South Lake Tahoe, Town of Truckee, and Tahoe Transportation District. The maintainer will build, maintain, and fund it. Recurring costs must stay under $20/month, ideally lower. AI summaries do not need prepublication human review.

This records the initial direction; the later web-first decision overrides alerts as an MVP requirement. The agencies, budget, ownership, and automatic publication policy remain accepted.

## Consequences
Notifications and subscription management are MVP work. Automated validation, source provenance, correction controls, deduplication, and cost limits are essential. An editorial approval queue is not a launch dependency. Digest cadence, stack, and vendors remain undecided.

## Revisit when
Measured cost, unreliable sources, or beta feedback make the scope unsustainable. Scope changes should be explicit rather than silently dropping an agency or alerts.
