# 0002: Topic-focused webpages before alerts

Status: accepted
Date: 2026-10-19
Related ideas: IDEA-001, IDEA-002
Supersedes: the alert requirement in [0001](0001-mvp-scope.md)

## Context and decision
The maintainer narrowed the first release to information on a webpage users can check regularly. Defer alerts and subscriptions. Launch topic pages for Biking (bike/street infrastructure and safety), Transit, Housing, and Environmental Protection.

The three agencies, the maintainer's ownership, automatic AI publication, and sub-$20/month ceiling remain unchanged. Neutral editorial coverage remains a proposal rather than a confirmed preference.

## Consequences
Prioritize useful topic matching, upcoming-item ordering, visible freshness and changes, and bookmarkable pages. No subscriber database or email delivery infrastructure is needed for MVP. Preserve alerts in the backlog.

## Revisit when
Beta users return regularly and request notifications, and delivery can fit the operating budget.
