# 0003: Mostly neutral coverage with limited attributed commentary

Status: superseded by [0010: Single editorial voice](0010-single-editorial-voice.md)
Date: 2026-10-19
Related ideas: IDEA-001, IDEA-007

## Context and decision
Considered: fairly neutral coverage with room for clearly attributed commentary. This resolves the open editorial question in decision 0002.

## Implementation approach
Keep factual agenda summaries source-backed and mostly neutral. Allow optional, clearly attributed commentary notes alongside them. A person authors or explicitly approves these notes; automatic summarization must not invent positions. Highlighting issues can reflect editorial priorities while the topic pages retain all matching coverage.

## Consequences
Automatic factual publication remains unchanged and does not wait for commentary. Add only a small optional commentary field to the MVP; advocacy campaigns, polling, and coordinated submissions remain deferred. Readers should be able to distinguish the proposed agency action, factual context, and commentary.

## Revisit when
Beta feedback shows confusion about attribution or the distinction between coverage and commentary.
