# IDEA-001: Meeting explorer and trustworthy ingestion

Status: proposed
Stage: MVP

## Problem and audience
Residents need one place to find upcoming meetings and understand agenda items.

## Smallest useful version
A date-grouped list and meeting pages for a small agency set; cited summaries, official participation links, source versions, editable topics, automatic validation and failure queue, and later recording/minutes links.

## Evidence and dependencies
Origin: initial design; demand has not yet been validated. Agency audit, TTD reuse assessment, and an operator.

## Acceptance criteria
Core tasks and publication gates in the MVP build plan pass.

## Open questions
Which agencies and how much packet coverage?
