# IDEA-007: Polling and advocacy

Status: inbox
Stage: Participation experiments

## Problem and audience
Organizers may want to collect views and coordinate engagement.

## Smallest useful version
Separately labeled advocacy content; one narrowly scoped survey experiment before generalized polling.

## Evidence and dependencies
Origin: initial design; demand has not yet been validated. Editorial stance is settled in [0010](../decisions/0010-single-editorial-voice.md): one editorial voice and no attributed commentary; campaigns and polling remain deferred. These broader features still need consent for attribution, abuse handling, and a defined audience.

## Acceptance criteria
Readers can distinguish coverage from advocacy; survey reports explain sampling limitations and attribution choices.

## Open questions
Who speaks for the platform, and who owns campaigns?
