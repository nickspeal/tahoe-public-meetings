# IDEA-010: Live meeting discussion

Status: inbox
Stage: Participation experiments

## Problem and audience
Meeting viewers may benefit from shared context and later discussion notes.

## Smallest useful version
A moderated pilot for one meeting before persistent public chat.

## Evidence and dependencies
Origin: initial design; demand has not yet been validated. Named moderators, abuse reporting, retention rules, and sufficient live audience.

## Acceptance criteria
A moderator can remove abusive content; discussion is clearly separate from official minutes.

## Open questions
Is live chat more useful than asynchronous notes? Any sharing with officials requires a separate explicit policy.
