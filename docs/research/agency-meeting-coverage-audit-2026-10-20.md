## Result and limitations

The local export contains **108 meetings across 33 agencies/bodies**, 55 source documents, 278 agenda items, and 556 validated citation references. The included reproducible baseline fixture still has 19 meetings; this run added 89 distinct records to the local database. The dropdown remains limited to agencies with stored meetings. The original 42-row snapshot had nine entries with no imported meeting for the reasons above; subsequent registry decisions and candidate records are detailed in the follow-up section. Many additions are verified notices or documents whose agenda items still require parsing; this is not a comprehensive live feed.

Final checks: 63 backend tests, 15 frontend/calendar tests, clean Astro diagnostics, 118 built pages, and 2,254 checked internal links. A partial-ingestion defect found during capture was fixed: incoming cited items are no longer immediately invalidated along with older unprocessed items, and a later parse can restore previously invalidated evidence.

## Acquisition and verification

The registry's `officialUrl` and `sourceUrl` fields contain current official identities or operational source pages; legacy CSV values remain in `legacy` and retain their `unverified` labels. Sources were checked against official agency pages and archives. Native `curl` downloads used standard certificate verification; no TLS verification was disabled. Temporary agenda PDFs were removed from the acquisition scratch folders after text capture. No permanent PDF archive was created.

Tracked source-candidate manifests are `config/verified-agency-meetings-2026-10-20.json` and `config/verified-agency-meetings-2026-10-20-batch2.json` through `batch11.json`, plus the follow-up SLT Parks and TCPUD committee manifests linked above. They preserve source URLs for both successful and failed document fetches. The captured page text and document provenance are in ignored `data/live/` manifests: `lahontan-captured.json`, `state-parks-captured.json`, `sstma-captured.json`, and `fgc-captured.json`. Lahontan PDFs were captured separately by the root agent. PDFs are not committed.

Reproduce a bounded capture from a candidate manifest with:

```sh
python3 scripts/project.py worker init-db
python3 scripts/project.py worker capture config/verified-agency-meetings-2026-10-20-batch8.json --max-meetings 7 --max-documents 10
python3 scripts/project.py worker capture config/verified-agency-meetings-2026-10-20-batch9.json --max-meetings 1 --max-documents 1
python3 scripts/project.py worker capture config/verified-agency-meetings-2026-10-20-batch11.json --max-meetings 3 --max-documents 1
python3 scripts/project.py worker ingest-manifest data/live/state-parks-captured.json
python3 scripts/project.py worker ingest-manifest data/live/sstma-captured.json
python3 scripts/project.py worker ingest-manifest data/live/fgc-captured.json
python3 scripts/project.py refresh
```

The capture command inserts verified notices, preserves partial logistics, and marks agenda/document completeness conservatively. Manual captured manifests preserve extracted page text, source URLs, SHA-256, and fetch timestamps; notice-only rows retain explicit agenda review warnings. The source research did not add adapters or claim automated coverage. The dropdown filter was left unchanged; its existing behavior continues to show agencies with stored meetings.
