# Topic catalog and source-title audit

The current ordered catalog is [`packages/domain/topics.json`](../../packages/domain/topics.json): Biking, Transit, Housing, Short Term Rentals, Environment, Elections, and Hot Topics. The maintainer added Elections on October 20, 2026 ([decision 0011](../decisions/0011-claude-for-automated-analysis.md)). [Decision 0009](../decisions/0009-configurable-coverage-and-one-off-meetings.md) replaces the twelve-topic selection from decision 0008. See the [configuration guide](../../config/README.md) for patterns, inclusion relationships, manual tags, and reclassification commands.

The classifier uses each agenda item's title and limited TTD context. Citation excerpts may contain neighboring items and are retained as evidence, never searched for keywords. Every generated tag records rule, matched title span, configuration-sensitive classifier version, and citation. Reclassification preserves source versions, summaries, and independent human overrides. Retired overrides remain stored and are hidden from public output. Procedural entries remain untagged unless their titles identify a followed subject.

The 19-meeting, 243-item source fixture now yields biking 5, transit 16, housing 5, short-term-rentals 1, environment 10, and hot-topics 3 automatic assignments. Multi-topic items count more than once. These describe fixture coverage rather than agency-wide prevalence or measured turnout. The source-backed [topic expectations](../../tests/fixtures/topic-expectations.json) and configuration tests cover positive and negative cases.

TTD's “Cycle B” remains transit, never biking. Complete Streets is Biking. A public transit safety plan is Transit. A generic mobility hub is Transit; the named Incline Village hub or 771 Southwood address also matches Hot Topics. Vacation home rentals match Short Term Rentals and Housing. Road names in property descriptions do not alone match Biking. Topic subjects found only in supporting staff material may require manual tagging until richer item-bounded evidence is available. The [classification evaluation](topic-classification-evaluation-2026-10-20.md) compares these rules with Claude Haiku on titles and item-bounded agenda excerpts.
